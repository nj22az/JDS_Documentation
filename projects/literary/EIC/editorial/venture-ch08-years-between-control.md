# The Venture — Chapter Eight (`The Years Between`) Control

Status: final-control report. No structural rewrite required.

## Verdict

Keep the dual structure:

- Wapping receives Tom indirectly through paid-off sailors and Jack;
- Maggie refuses to manufacture a correspondence;
- Maria Mori independently builds the information network that will eventually expose Daniel Vale's continued existence.

This is useful breathing room between Tom's direct chapters and Amboyna.

## Required continuity

- Maggie receives no Tom letters after 1612.
- Jack carries no secret message.
- Maria Mori name applies throughout.
- Tom's Virginia period means reports placing him at Surat must begin only after 1617; the current 1614 paid-off report that has him `at the Company's door` may remain because it describes him in London, but any early Surat detail must be dated consistently.

## Wapping half — protect

Protect:
- food before questions;
- sailors' ordinary habits rather than heroic voyage summaries;
- Jack's return and refusal of another voyage;
- Maggie asking whether Tom is alive, not whether he remembers her;
- the recovered Yusuf fragment showing how easily the labourer's name is lost;
- Maggie refusing to send a page merely to provoke an answer.

## Wapping half — reduce

The chapter still occasionally frames Maggie as conducting an alternative moral archive.

Reduce or recast:
- `Maggie is not collecting evidence for Tom's acquittal.`
- repeated `both parts / both accounts / contradictions` explanations;
- `The Company sends fair copies. The sea sends contradictions.` if similar formulations already survive elsewhere;
- extended explanation of silence as an `entry`.

Maggie's actions already show the distinction.

## Maria half — protect

Protect:
- second teak desk to Aminah;
- paying work rather than owning people;
- duplicate routes;
- source protection;
- missing hands after Banda;
- Rafi's family payment;
- forged-seal incident;
- Daniel Vale first appearing through the rice margin note and naming porters.

These are concrete steps in Maria Mori's earned professional development.

## Maria half — reduce

The later network prose becomes increasingly abstract and aphoristic.

Target for final prose compression:
- `The Dutch do not want cloves. They want the sentence all the cloves to become true.` — strong but use only if no adjacent paragraph makes same point.
- repeated declarations about power, trust, names and paper;
- `Trust is an intersecting web...` explanatory paragraph after the forged-seal incident;
- lines where Iskandar asks the exact philosophical question required for Maria to state the theme.

Prefer the operating rules themselves: two routes, different keys, source names withheld, family fees paid first, altered seal.

## Daniel setup

The rice-account introduction is excellent because Daniel becomes interesting through one behaviour: he names the porters and records where the sacks actually went.

Preserve this. Do not add a moral label to him before the reader meets him directly.

## Pass condition

The chapter should feel like nine years in which information arrives imperfectly and people continue living, not like a lecture on the counter-ledger conducted simultaneously in London and Asia.
