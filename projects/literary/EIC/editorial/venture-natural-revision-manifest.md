## Global name rule

All revised Book One prose uses:

**Maria Mori**

`Maria` is her Christian/baptismal given name. `Mori` is her Japanese birth-family name. Do not imply membership in the historical Mōri clan without a later explicit author decision.

## Global craft rule

The new opening is the tonal benchmark:

- people before thesis;
- setting before symbolism;
- work knowledge before exposition;
- objects before explanation;
- institutional language contaminates characters gradually rather than serving as the narrator's default voice.

## Final assembly warning

The repository contains historical experiments with names, sent letters, chapter counts and alternate rewrites. File recency alone is not sufficient to choose between them. This manifest governs the natural revision until superseded by an explicit later author decision.
