# The Venture — Parallel Audit Synthesis

Status: accepted integration plan after independent A/B/C audits.

## Accepted findings

The three audits agree on the following:

1. The Step 4 Chapter One redesign is the correct foundation. Do not restore a formal court, jury, tribunal, collective verdict, or mythic Wapping justice mechanism.
2. The largest immediate continuity problem is Chapter Two, which still remembers the old near-lynching/trial version and contradicts Hendricks's presence in the new Chapter One.
3. Maria Mori is the governing name for all revised Book One material. `de Sousa` is deprecated.
4. Maria Mori's 1603 characterization must show intelligence, restraint and tactical observation without giving her the fully developed ledger philosophy of her later teak-desk years.
5. Maggie should act as keeper, provider and exact questioner, not as the novel's philosophical magistrate.
6. Tom's first moral act remains standing between Rook and Maria before he knows the truth. His willingness to sign anyway must remain equally visible.
7. `The Same Ink` stays in the novel but loses explicit future-book narration and repeated statements of its own thesis.
8. The seven unsent Tom-to-Maggie letters are the current repository canon; the eighth letter remains Daniel Vale walked through the door. They should remain mostly off-page.
9. `Last Orders` keeps its structure but should trust the thimble, pie, papers and Joan more, explaining them less.
10. The `Wapping Twelve` may survive only as optional editorial/reference history, not as the narrative identity of the people in the room.

## Craft rules accepted for integration

- Ledger language is a contaminating vocabulary that enters different characters at different rates; it is not the default prose language.
- Paired truths are reserved for major moral pivots rather than used as a recurrent sentence template.
- Historical information should enter through work, bodies, cargo, money, documents, rumour and practical obstacles wherever possible.
- After a physical object carries an emotional beat, the following explanatory paragraph is presumed expendable until proven necessary.
- Wapping is rendered materially: tide, mud, stairs, timber, rent, ale, work, multilingual dock life and changing buildings.

## Integration order

### Integration 1 — Opening sequence

- correct `Maria de Sousa` to `Maria Mori` in the Step 4 Chapter One proposal;
- rewrite Chapter Two's Maria/Hendricks half so it follows the new Chapter One rather than the old trial;
- retain Anne Bell's practical search/resistance sequence with only continuity edits;
- check the first pages of `Maria's Passage East` so Maria's developing paper-literacy is earned rather than already complete.

### Integration 2 — Structural middle

- revise 1612 rupture transitions;
- revise `The Same Ink` for restraint and historical verification;
- check Chapter Five movement/length without automatically splitting it;
- preserve unsent-letter continuity through Surat/Batavia.

### Integration 3 — Ending and motif restraint

- revise Rook/Maria continuity in `The South Land`;
- compress explanatory object prose in `Last Orders`;
- retire final `Wapping Twelve` narrative callout;
- update character/motif/reference documents after prose stabilises.

### Integration 4 — Final controls

- dates, ages, voyages, knowledge states, names and objects;
- historical source verification;
- complete prose pass;
- cover-to-cover read as one novel.

## Governing test

The book should remain capable of making its argument after most explicit statements of that argument have been removed.
