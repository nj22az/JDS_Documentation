# The Venture — Chapter Nine (`Amboyna`) Control

Status: final-control report. Architecture retained; historical/prose limits applied during assembly.

## Verdict

Protect the Daniel-centred chapter.

Its progression is strong:

Daniel seeks advancement -> becomes useful through exact records -> begins buying his own paper -> notices closing signs -> Towerson cannot act on six individually reasonable signals -> Daniel is arrested -> water torture -> harvested confession -> unexplained transfer to Batavia.

Do not rewrite it into a history lecture or a detective plot.

## Historical controls

### Execution framework

The revised book uses the historical figure of **ten English, ten Japanese and one Portuguese executed** after the Amboyna proceedings.

Daniel is fictional and survives. His survival must not imply that every other accused Englishman was executed or that he was literally the sole surviving English accused man.

Therefore revise the line:
`He is the only Englishman leaving Amboyna by water that week.`

Prefer a narrower fictional observation, e.g.:
`He sees no other English prisoner put aboard with him.`

That preserves Daniel's isolation without claiming a complete historical survivor list.

### Disputed conspiracy

Daniel may say and believe `There is no plot.`

The narrator should not convert his belief into omniscient proof. Modern scholarship treats the surviving records and later national narratives critically.

### Torture

The cloth-and-water sequence may remain. It is grounded in the historical controversy and is presented through Daniel's experience.

Avoid adding later English propaganda details merely for horror.

### Towerson

Towerson remains a real historical person viewed through fictional Daniel's admiration. `The best man I have served under` is Daniel's judgement, not narrator biography.

## Craft controls

### `The Margin`

Excellent. Keep Daniel's inability to initial a number he personally watched be false.

The line distinguishing bravery from inability to sign false numbers may remain; it defines Daniel rather than the book's entire thesis.

### Broker / Maria setup

The anonymous broker's rate and the line about buying paper with his own coin are useful, but Towerson currently explains paper ownership rather neatly.

Compress his speech. Daniel can infer the consequence after paying for the paper himself.

### `The Audit of Small Things`

Strong. Keep the six individually reasonable signs.

The phrase `a door being bolted from the other side` is concrete enough to survive.

Avoid adding narrator statements that Maria is simultaneously reaching exactly the same sentence. Her network can independently converge later in Chapter Eight/Eleven without perfect thematic simultaneity.

### Torture / signature

Protect:
- pen writes some answers and ignores others;
- cloth/water procedure;
- Daniel loses count;
- Towerson through the wall;
- fair confession made from Daniel's true details;
- Daniel signs because his body has learned the cost of refusal;
- Nell's name remains unsaid.

These scenes require very little added explanation.

Potential reductions:
- `Truth is being transcribed backward.` if adjacent pen behaviour already makes it clear;
- `They are making him into a page.` if the image is repeated elsewhere;
- `the room's parting gift` explanation after his lifelong attention to pens.

### Transfer to Batavia

Keep the single word `Batavia` and Daniel never learning why he was transferred.

Remove/reduce the abstract `two columns / cargo` explanation if the doorway exchange already carries it.

## Continuity with revised 1626

Daniel's later public account must use the same factual limits:
- his own water torture is certain;
- Towerson's suffering is primarily heard through the wall;
- he does not personally witness all twenty-one executions;
- he may later learn the execution count from records/other survivors;
- he does not know why he was sent to Batavia.

## Pass condition

The reader should leave Amboyna knowing exactly what Daniel experienced and still understanding that a traumatised witness is not an omniscient historian.
