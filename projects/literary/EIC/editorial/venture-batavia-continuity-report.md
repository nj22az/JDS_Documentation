# The Venture — Batavia Continuity & Prose Check

Status: final-control report. The chapter's architecture is retained; targeted line edits required during assembly.

## Chapter

`manuscript-editorial/19-1625-batavia-merged.md`

## Verdict

Structurally strong. Do **not** rewrite the chapter wholesale.

Its best movement remains:

Amboyna reaches Surat as competing accounts -> Tom keeps names and ten blank Japanese lines -> a retained English factor appears in private paperwork -> Maria Mori independently finds Daniel through the provision trail -> Tom moves east toward the entry -> Tom and Maria meet -> Daniel is released to a deck -> practical accommodations teach the body that not every order is a threat -> Daniel travels west.

This supports the revised 1626 chapter well.

## Required continuity corrections

### 1. Maria Mori

Replace any remaining `Maria de Sousa` with **Maria Mori**.

### 2. Amboyna executions

Keep the chapter's count of ten English dead and ten Japanese dead. Where the Portuguese victim is relevant to an account, include him; do not imply the executed group was only English + Japanese.

The revised 1626 chapter states: ten English, ten Japanese, one Portuguese.

### 3. Letters

Tom's post-1612 letters to Maggie are unsent.

The paragraph where he places a clean sheet beside the Amboyna papers and folds the blank sheet is compatible with the unsent-letter arc and may remain.

Do not import material from files named `sent-letters-rework`.

### 4. Japanese names

Tom's ten blank lines remain strong.

Maria's later recovery of the names remains unexplained to Tom. Preserve that. It demonstrates her network without an exposition scene.

## Required craft reductions

### A. Paired-truth commentary

Cut/recast:
- `Both facts stand.`
- `Paper order is not justice...`

The chief factor's practical need to redirect ships while men are dead already carries the contradiction.

### B. Machine language

Cut/recast:
- `One choice left in a man's hands does not stop the machine...`
- `He has corrected the machine for twenty years...`

Use Tom's observed actions and office choices instead.

### C. `grief and use`

The section entitled `Grief and Use` may retain its events but should not repeatedly name the mechanism. Carter is not physically present in Surat; the prose should not summon his future 1626 response as though history is arranged around the motif.

### D. Maria/Tom recognition

Cut the explicit formula:
`One long look. Not love. Recognition.`

Their recognition is already clear from posture, Wapping memory and the correction of Daniel's name.

Also reduce the paragraph that has Tom remember Maria as a woman `standing behind a bar she never stood behind`. It is elegant but dreamlike in a chapter being moved toward material realism.

### E. Pelican mythology

Maria's line `There is a room in London that reads accounts truly` should be made more specific and human.

Recommended sense:

> There is a keeper in Wapping who kept Bell's page when carrying it would have killed me. If she is still there, take him to her.

This makes the connection Maggie, not a magical room.

### F. Genever callback

The shared genever bottle may remain, but the surrounding prose should not say the room becomes full of everyone who carried the drink across history. Let Tom recognise the taste and leave it there.

### G. Daniel's deck recovery

This is some of the best material in Book One. Protect:
- Tom reading before signing;
- Tom stepping out of the doorway;
- `Can you walk that far? Then that is all we are doing today.`
- chair turned toward opening;
- food portions adjusted without interrogation;
- covered cup on the left;
- lantern catch repaired;
- screen left a hand's width open.

Cut/recast the summary lines that explain these behaviours as `true books`, `accounts`, or `a deck has better manners than a court`.

The practical acts are enough.

## Historical alignment

The chapter's use of competing Dutch/English accounts is compatible with the revised Amboyna approach, provided the narrator does not declare every contested claim settled.

The English Company's desire for a usable anti-Dutch account should become more visible as the chapter approaches London, which the revised 1626 chapter then pays off.

## Pass condition

The chapter passes when Maria and Tom meet because they have both independently arrived at Daniel—not because the novel needs two thematic systems to intersect—and when Daniel's recovery is understood through care practices rather than explained as an alternative theory of record-keeping.
