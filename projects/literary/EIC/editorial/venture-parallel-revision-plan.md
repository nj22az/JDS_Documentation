# The Venture — Parallel Revision Plan

Branch: `agent/venture-natural-opening`

## Purpose

Replace the original 20-step mostly sequential revision workflow with coordinated editorial workstreams. Independent audits are performed against the same manuscript state before prose is changed, reducing confirmation bias and preventing one edit from hiding another problem.

## Workstream A — Opening / Character Integrity

Runs together:
- Step 5: second-pass Chapter One review
- Step 6: Maggie introduction
- Step 7: Tom opening arc
- Step 8: Maria Mori and Silas Rook
- Step 9: residual Wapping Twelve / courtroom machinery

Deliverable: one opening-integrity report, followed by a consolidated Chapter One revision.

## Workstream B — Structural / Narrative Flow

Runs together:
- Step 10: transition from 1603 into the wider world
- Step 11: The Same Ink
- Step 15: chapter pacing and length
- Step 16: Maggie–Tom rupture and letters
- Step 17: ending review

Deliverable: one structural-spine report covering causal flow, emotional rhythm and chapter balance.

## Workstream C — Whole-Book Craft Audit

Runs together:
- Step 12: historical exposition
- Step 13: repeated motifs
- Step 14: dialogue and character voice

Deliverable: cross-book issue register with locations, severity and recommended treatment.

## Workstream D — Final Integration

Must follow A–C:
- Step 18: full continuity pass
- Step 19: prose pass
- Step 20: complete novel read

These remain sequential because they depend on the revised manuscript being stable.

## Revised schedule

Instead of 16 remaining sequential steps, the work is now grouped into five phases:

1. Opening Integrity — A
2. Structural Spine — B
3. Whole-Book Craft Audit — C
4. Integration — continuity + prose
5. Final Novel Read

A, B and C may be analysed independently against the same manuscript state. Changes are not merged into the prose until the findings are synthesised, which avoids conflicting edits.

## Reporting rule

Each workstream report states:
1. what was examined;
2. what was found;
3. what should change;
4. what should explicitly remain untouched;
5. downstream consequences.

No deployed/live canon changes are made during these audits.
