# The Venture — Chapter Seven (`Tom at Surat`) Continuity & Prose Control

Status: final-control report. Chapter architecture retained after chronology repair.

## Required chapter identity

Change the chapter span from **1614–1622** to **1617–1622**.

The new Chapter Six, `The Same Ink`, now occupies Tom's post-Jack movement from 1614 through his Virginia service, 1616 return, Gravesend in 1617 and decision to take the Surat berth.

## Sections now superseded

### Old Section I — `The Letter Waiting`

Remove from Chapter Seven.

It repeats:
- Tom returning to the pay office after Jack;
- his mother's death letter;
- closing her rooms;
- signing the Surat berth directly.

These events are now handled, with corrected chronology, in `book-one-ch06-the-same-ink-natural-revision.md`.

Chapter Seven should begin with Tom arriving/returning to Surat in 1617.

### Old Section V — `Orphan's Work`

Remove or reduce to a very short memory fragment.

The long retrospective of Tom's father's death and his mother's later death now duplicates material already carried by Chapters Four and Six and stalls the 1617–1622 forward movement.

Protect only details that are genuinely needed later:
- father's spare whistle;
- Tom's memory of parental work and practical speech;
- no invented final words for his mother.

Do not retell the full death-room sequence here.

### Old Section X — `The Letter Not Sent`

Replace with the governing chronology in:
`editorial/tom-unsent-letters-revised.md`.

The existing sequence places early letters before/around the old 1614 Surat start and therefore no longer fits.

Book One rule remains: seven letters written, none sent; the 1622 `You were right` letter is burned; Daniel is the eighth living letter in 1626.

## Sections to protect

### `What the Company Keeps`

Strong working-life material. Keep:
- boat crew refusing the bad bar;
- Tom agreeing with local expertise;
- London commendation omitting the boatmen's names.

Reduce narrator explanation that tells us exactly how the Company is changing Tom.

### `The Exact Instrument`

Strong contradiction between safety improvements and increased throughput.

Remove the phrase-level thesis repetition:
- `Every correction strengthens the machine that required correcting.`

Let cracked beam -> safer landing -> more bales; corrected slops -> men signing again; load-line refusal -> larger vessel demonstrate the point.

### `The Missing Bale`

Protect almost entirely.

Yusuf and the one-stroke copying error are excellent because the institutional danger is concrete.

Cut/compress the concluding abstract summary:
- `A man is nearly destroyed. A system learns...`

Tom later remembering `the missing-bale case` rather than Yusuf is sufficient and more painful without commentary.

### `The Lone Machine` cotton-order material

Keep the logistics and blue-cotton echo, but rename the movement and remove repeated `machine/fits him` explanation.

Recommended section title: **The Large Order** or **The Warehouse**.

### `The Door`

Protect.

Tom's habit of never sitting with his back to a door and the rice-thief boy are strong body-memory continuity from 1603.

Keep the line that rooms do not remember for men; people bring memory into them. It supports the natural-Wapping revision.

### `The Signature`

Protect the blank-declaration conflict.

Remove the final sentence `The machine has learned from his refusal.` The new estimated-quantities clause already demonstrates that result.

### `Shore Leave`

Protect the cobbler/awning sequence. It shows Tom unable to stop repairing without explaining him through Company vocabulary.

Compress the final paired-summary language (`sea carried home... home carried back... neither releases...`) if needed; the repaired boots/awning/return to river already carry it.

### `The Thimble`

Retain Tom's hand searching for brass by habit, but reduce the dream/version sequence. In particular, remove Maria appearing in dark silk behind Maggie's bar: Tom has not yet met adult Maria and the dream makes continuity feel authored.

### `The Echo`

Keep Tom changing his method with the frozen boy: voice/instruction instead of automatic force.

Remove:
- the full stacked list of father/Maggie/Bell/Maria voices beneath the order;
- explicit `woman he loves` wording;
- forward-looking `other ledgers are closing around names...`.

The final sentence `The sea repeats nothing exactly. It carries the sound forward changed.` may remain as a rare chapter-ending aphorism.

## New opening recommendation

After the epigraph, begin directly in 1617:

Tom returns to Surat with Virginia still physically present in his habits: he asks for two tallies at landing before anyone there knows why; the same procedure dismissed in Virginia now solves an actual warehouse problem. Do not explain the symmetry immediately.

## New epigraph direction

Retire:
`A machine does not need to hate a man. It only needs him to fit.`

This is too close to the old governing thesis.

Prefer a work-centred line from Tom, for example:

> *Read it again. The error is usually where everybody agreed too quickly.*

The exact final epigraph can be chosen during assembly.

## Pass condition

Chapter Seven should feel like five years of Tom becoming exceptionally good at Surat work after trying a different ocean—not like a second beginning that erases the Virginia chapter.
